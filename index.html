About these standards
  • Oregon "A": 2026–2027 Oregon Swimming time standards. Ages 8 & under, 9, 10, 11, 12, 13, 14 and 15 & over each have their own cuts. From the sheet: 15 & Over standards for Short Course Championships are equal to the 14-year-old standards.
  • YMCA: 2027 Northwest Region YMCA Qualifying Times (yards and short course meters only). 11–12 and 13–14 swimmers also see the 11–14 distance events (200 Back/Breast/Fly, 400 IM, 1000/1650 Y, 800/1500 M).
  • YMCA Nationals: 2027 National YMCA Short Course Swimming Championship qualifying times (yards, short course meters and long course meters). These are single cuts for each gender with no age groups. The qualifying period is February 1, 2026 to the meet's entry date. From the sheet: a swimmer who qualifies in the 1000 or 1650 Free may also enter the other one with an alternate time (Women: 10:49.99 Y / 12:01.49 SCM / 12:15.92 LCM for 1000Y/800M; 18:14.99 Y / 20:15.44 SCM / 20:39.75 LCM for 1650Y/1500M. Men: 10:05.99 / 11:12.65 / 11:26.10 and 16:55.99 / 18:47.75 / 19:10.30).
  • A time equal to or faster than the cut counts as qualifying.
  • Each meet sets its own age-up date. Pick the age your swimmer will be for that championship meet.
  • Relays are not included. Always confirm with the official meet announcement or a coach. Times are saved only on this device and in this browser (a home-screen app and Safari keep separate copies).
